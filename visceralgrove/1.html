Chapter 1

It's dark.

It's night... Already?

The wooden floorboards are stiff beneath you. You're sitting in a room.

It's dark...

Too dark to see anything...

Flowing, from in front, like a wave, a sudden breeze of wind brushes against your face.
Which, traveling around you, provides a sense of defining shape to the rest of your body.

But... you don't hear it,
The sound wind makes...?

When you finally, very cautiously, open your eyes...

You see nothing.

Isn't this still my house?
Isn't this still my bedroom?
I wasn't asleep that long was I?

You can't see anything.
Whatever houses you, in this state, it's seemingly boundless.
Infinite nothingness.
Silent...

It's suffocatingly silent.

Home was swept away, right under your feet, in your most vulnerable moment.

Overwhelming dread begins to crawl,
it seeps into you,
claiming you,

The darkness knows you, comforting you, when there's nothing else.

No one else.

Nothing.

Today was a lot...
Thank god it's over.

You close your eyes.

Or did you open them?

It's hard to tell...

Maybe just stare out there forever.
Eyes open.
Eyes closed.
Darkness doesn't falter.

Sight doesn't matter,
Hide away.

I should have just stayed in my room.

I should stay here.

Another breeze, seemingly from nowhere...
this time it's noticably colder...
its wispy, soft voice echoes throughout your head.

"What can you do for them?"
"What can you do for yourself?"

"What if what they want is someone that doesn't exist?"

"Wouldn't it be better if you were somebody else?"

What if I wasn't myself.

Tears well, your eyes burn

I'm somebody else.
